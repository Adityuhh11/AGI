# macOS M2: clipboard history and paste

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-macos-design.md` (5.3 and section 8). The shared history, filters and the order of a paste were already behind interfaces; M2 fills the Mac leaves.

1. **Source** (`clipboard/source_darwin.go`, `pasteboard_darwin.m`): the change counter of `NSPasteboard` (polled every 250 ms as on Windows), the text, the private markers of nspasteboard.org
   (concealed, transient, auto-generated), and who copied: the application that has the keyboard, since a Mac does not say. This program's own copies are known by their counter (`NoteOwnCopy`),
   because with a bar that never activates the program the "frontmost app" would otherwise be taken for the owner of a paste.
2. **Paster** (`paste_darwin.go`): ⌘V through `CGEvent`, with the key found for "v" on the layout in use. Before anything is typed: not while a password field has the keyboard
   (`IsSecureEventInputEnabled`), and only with the Accessibility permission, which macOS' own dialog asks for once. Without it the text is copied and the bar says so (`ErrNoAccessibility`).
   The shared `Paster` field `Blocked` became `Allowed`, returning its reason, since the two systems have different ones.
3. **Settings**: a Mac-only paragraph on the Accessibility permission; the program list says "app".
4. **Tests**: the source against the real pasteboard through `pbcopy` on the Mac runner. Typing into another app is for the friend (checklist 4).
