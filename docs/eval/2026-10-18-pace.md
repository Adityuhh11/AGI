# What the indexing speeds do (packaging block F, 2026-10-18)

The real engine (llama.cpp on the RTX 4050 through Vulkan), `Sample Data/` indexed from empty at each duty (431 chunks: text, PDF pages
and images), with `nvidia-smi` sampled every 0.2 s and a search sent every 0.4 s meanwhile. The duty is the share of the time the
indexer may keep the GPU busy (`pace.py`: after a bulk embedding call that took `t`, wait `t * (1/duty - 1)`).

| Speed | duty | time to index | GPU utilisation, mean | samples over 10% | search p50 / p95 while indexing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fast | 1.0 | 17.6 s | 65% | 90% | 93 ms / 211 ms |
| Balanced | 0.5 | 33.4 s (1.9 times) | 27% | 37% | 41 ms / 174 ms |
| Gentle | 0.2 | 103.5 s (5.9 times) | 16% | 23% | 36 ms / 216 ms |

What it says:

- The knob behaves: half the duty takes about twice as long, a fifth takes about six times (a little over five, because every call
  has a fixed cost the rest period does not shorten). The names and the words in the window ("about twice as long", "about six times
  as long") are these numbers.
- Flat out the GPU is busy 65% of the time, not 100%: chunking, PNG encoding and the HTTP round trips are on the CPU.
- Gentle's 16% includes the test's own searches (224 of them, a few percent of GPU time each); the indexer's share is smaller still.
- **Searching gets faster when indexing is slower**: the median search during indexing falls from 93 ms to 41 and 36 ms, because the
  queries no longer queue behind bulk calls. A person who wants to keep searching while the index builds should choose Gentle.

The default stays Balanced: the index builds in about twice the time and the laptop is not pinned.

## In the app

Driven with real clicks in a clean profile (config: file search on, Gentle, `Sample Data` as the folder, an empty index): the File search
page shows "Indexing Sample Data: 41 / 96 files" (and the folder's own line, "Indexing: 41 of 96 files") while it runs; **Pause indexing**
held the count at 54 of 96 across eight seconds and the page said "Indexing is paused. Search still works on what is indexed."; **Resume
indexing** went on from 54; the end state was "Up to date: 431 chunks in 96 files" and "96 files searchable". With the old engine (one
installed before this build, which has no pace routes) the same folder indexed at full speed, 71 of 96 files in about 12 s, which is why
the page hides the pause controls while an update is waiting.
